## Intent

Create a second Vibe Roaster product promo that feels decisively more kinetic
than the first film. Prompt fragments are pulled into an invisible judgment
machine; the four personality axes hit like verdicts; Builder is scanned into
view; all sixteen types arrive as a directed character explosion. The product
should feel playful, sharp, and self-aware rather than like a dashboard tour.

## Assets

- `../../assests/characters-vibe-types/` — canonical transparent artwork for all sixteen personalities.
- `../../assests/readme/vibe-roast-16-types-banner.webp` — canonical ensemble and wordmark composition.
- `../../assests/screenshots/roast-result-hero.jpg` — current product result view.
- `../../media/promo/` — V1 timing and visual reference only; V2 must not reproduce its slideshow-like movement.

## Customizations

- Use proven motion blueprints instead of generic opacity-and-translate animation.
- Make motion seek-safe with path attraction, FLIP queues, masks, foreground/background parallax, and decisive overshoot.
- Build an original local sound bed whose impacts land on the four axis verdicts and the final logo sting.
- Design the 9:16 social master first; preserve a composition strategy that can be adapted to 16:9 after approval.

## Notes

- No narration and no external licensed music.
- English on-screen copy for broad shareability.
- Preserve the existing character illustration style, cream/dark/mint/coral palette, and Great Vibes wordmark.
- Avoid device mockups, feature-list cards, conventional SaaS screen tours, and motion that freezes after the first second.
