## Video direction

Palette follows `frame.md`: warm cream is the editorial evidence field, ink is
the verdict voice, mint is the structural scan/current, coral is the single
voltage hit, and navy is reserved for the intake/terminal machine. Display copy
uses the display role, behavioral labels use mono, and the Great Vibes asset is
reserved for the final wordmark.

Motion grammar is one-way and consequential: elements are pulled, classified,
materialized, or expelled; nothing floats merely to look alive. Use smooth
long-tail settles (`power3` default), velocity blur only during genuinely fast
travel, and deterministic depth/FLIP paths. With no narration, visible text and
percussion hits are the cue rail: every phrase, axis, tag, and character wave
arrives on its own cue across the full shot, especially its back half. Settled
states hold still; only the final caret and an extremely subtle machine jitter
may remain active.

This landscape edition is a native 16:9 re-layout: evidence and payoff share
the width instead of placing the portrait master inside a letterboxed frame.
Frame 3 supplies the short recognition hold before the cast climax. Frame 5
holds the command long enough to copy. Never use slideshow behavior
(front-loaded entrance then freeze), screensaver behavior (independent ambient
floating), generic SaaS cards, purple-blue AI gradients, device chrome, lazy
breathing, repeated camera pushes, or default bounce easing. Keep important
content inside the top 83% safe region.
